Frequently asked.

Answers are based on the currently published official information. Details can change, so check Rockstar’s site for updates.

When does GTA VI release?

Rockstar lists November 19, 2026. Its support page says the game releases at midnight local time on that date.

Which platforms are confirmed?

Rockstar currently lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Is a PC version confirmed?

A PC version is not listed on the current official GTA VI game page. No PC release date is stated there.

Who are the main characters?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are the two central protagonists described on Rockstar’s official page.

Where does the game take place?

The game is set in Leonida, with Vice City as a key location.
